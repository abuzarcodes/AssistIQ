AssistIQ is an AI-powered customer support SaaS that helps businesses create, customize, and deploy AI chatbots to handle customer queries using their own knowledge base, with options for human support when AI cannot resolve an issue.

It combines a no-code setup experience with developer-friendly customization, allowing businesses to deploy support through a public chat page or website embed without building a chatbot from scratch.

Key features:

- AI-powered support: Answers customer questions using business-specific knowledge and RAG.
- Knowledge Base Management: Upload documents, manage knowledge chunks, and keep information up to date.
- Bot Customization: Configure instructions, models, appearance, fallback behavior, and responses.
- Human Handoff: Transfer conversations to human support agents when needed.
- Support Inbox: Manage conversations, assignments, and customer interactions.
- Bot Experience Studio: Create public chat pages and website embeds with deployment-level analytics.
- Multi-workspace architecture: Organize bots and support operations across workspaces.

Tech stack: Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Node.js, Express, Prisma, PostgreSQL, Python, FastAPI, LangChain, and vector search.

Core vision: Make AI customer support accessible to businesses of all sizes through a simple, customizable, and reliable platform that combines automated assistance with human support when necessary.
